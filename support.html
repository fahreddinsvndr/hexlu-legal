Welcome to the help page for Hexlu. Quick answers are below; for everything else just write to us.

Contact us

Send a message to fahreddin@pairo.cloud and mention your device model, system version and the game version (printed at the bottom of Settings). Replies usually take a few days.

Common questions

I reinstalled the game. How do I get my purchase back?

Go to the shop and choose Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID or Google account that paid. Remove Ads comes back immediately. Coins are consumed when spent, so there is nothing to restore for them.

What does Remove Ads do?

It is a one-time purchase that switches off the banner and the ads between levels for good, on every device that uses the same store account. It does not change how the levels play.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Rewarded ads are only offered for extras such as doubling the coins from a level, the daily gift or a hint, and you can always decline them.

Can I play Hexlu in my own language?

The game starts in your device language whenever it is one of the 20 we offer. To use a different one, go to Settings, tap Language and pick it; it switches straight away.

Can I play without telling colours apart?

Yes. Beams and lanterns always carry a pattern and a symbol as well as their colour, and Settings has colour-blind options. If a level is still hard to read, tell us which one.

What is the player number and where is it?

It is the random code the game gave your install, listed in Settings under About. Data requests need it, and it lets us find your crash reports.

I got a new phone. Is my progress safe?

It does not travel by itself. With no accounts, progress, stars and coins exist only on the device. Restoring a full device backup (iCloud or Android) can bring them back, while deleting the game wipes them.

Coins or hints are missing

Both are kept on the device, so a reinstall or cleared app data starts them from zero. Remove Ads can be restored in the shop. If you believe a purchase never arrived, e-mail us the Apple or Google receipt.

I am stuck on a level

Hint shows one useful move, but a level finished with a hint earns at most two stars. Phase Lock freezes the beam trail of one phase (Dawn, Noon or Dusk) as a ghost so you can plan the others; it never changes the solution.

Something crashed or looks wrong

Choose Report a problem in Settings to send us the latest activity trail, or e-mail a description and the level number. Installing the newest version cures most problems.

Delete my data

  • Send an e-mail to fahreddin@pairo.cloud titled "Delete my data" that includes the player number from Settings.
  • Statistics, crash reports and purchase records tied to that number are erased, and we confirm within 30 days.
  • Progress on the device disappears when you delete the game; you can also reset the advertising ID in your device settings.

Game version

You will find the version at the bottom of Settings. Please mention it in your message.
