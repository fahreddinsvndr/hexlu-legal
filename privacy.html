Hexlu ("the game") is a stained-glass light puzzle published by Pairo ("we", "us"). This page lists what information the game touches on Android phones and tablets and on iPhone and iPad, who receives it, and what you can decide.

At a glance

  • No account, no sign-in: the game never asks for a name, an e-mail address, a phone number or photos.
  • Progress, stars, coins and settings live on your device and nowhere else.
  • Google Firebase gives us usage statistics, crash reports and a few remotely adjustable settings, so we can keep the game steady and tune its levels.
  • Google AdMob shows the ads. Where the law demands it you are asked for consent first; on iPhone and iPad the system also asks before the advertising identifier may be used.
  • Apple or Google handles each purchase and RevenueCat records it. Card and bank details never reach us.
  • Your data is not sold for money, and the game has no use for your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or notifications.

Information the game handles

Player number and device facts

The first time you start Hexlu it draws a random 8-character player number that has no link to you or your hardware. Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) receives that number along with the device model, system version, language, app version and memory size, and RevenueCat uses it as an anonymous customer ID.

Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

Events are logged when you begin or complete a level, collect stars, use a Hint or Phase Lock, open the daily puzzle, move between screens, change a setting, see an ad or buy something. We study them in bulk to spot levels that are too hard or too easy and screens that confuse people.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When Hexlu crashes or hits a serious error, Crashlytics receives the technical details, the condition of the device and the last entries of a short activity trail made of screen names and button taps (never anything you type). Choosing "Report a problem" sends the same trail on purpose.

Settings fetched at launch (Firebase Remote Config)

On start-up the game pulls a handful of values from Firebase, such as the oldest app version still supported and how frequently ads may be shown. To tell installs apart when delivering them, Firebase assigns an installation identifier.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Hexlu shows banner and between-level ads, plus rewarded ads you can choose to watch, all from Google AdMob. To deliver and measure them Google gets the advertising identifier of your device (on iPhone and iPad only when you permit tracking), your IP address, a rough location worked out from it, and how you react to the ads. Your consent answer is stored on your device by Google's consent tool. If you have bought Remove Ads, no banner or between-level ads appear.

Purchases (RevenueCat, Apple, Google)

Apple or Google collects the payment when you buy coins or Remove Ads. RevenueCat gets the receipt, the product, your player number, country and IP address, which lets it confirm the purchase, switch Remove Ads on and bring it back when you ask.

What never leaves your device

Level progress, stars, the coin balance, hints, the state of the daily puzzle and your settings sit in the game's local storage. No server of ours holds a copy, so we cannot read them, and they do not follow you to a new phone on their own.

What we use it for

  • Running the game: remote values, unlocking purchases, update notices.
  • Keeping it steady: crash reports and activity trails.
  • Making it better: combined figures on levels, hints and screens.
  • Paying for it: ads that Google measures and guards against fraud.

Consent and your choices

  • Consent message. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other places that require it, Google shows a consent message before any ad loads. Change your answer whenever you like under Settings, Privacy options.
  • iPhone and iPad. iOS asks whether Hexlu may track you across other companies' apps and sites. Decline and ads still appear, just without the advertising identifier. Change it later in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Android. Under Settings, Google, Ads you can reset or delete your advertising ID or switch off ad personalisation.
  • Remove Ads. This one-time purchase ends banner and between-level ads. Rewarded ads stay your choice and skipping them costs nothing.
  • Reports. A problem report is only ever sent if you decide to send it.

Retention

Firebase Analytics keeps event data for at most 14 months. Crashlytics keeps crash reports for roughly 90 days. RevenueCat holds purchase records while they are needed to restore purchases and satisfy bookkeeping rules, or until you ask for deletion. Google handles advertising data under its own terms.

Sharing and transfers

Information goes only to the providers named on this page: Google (Firebase and AdMob) and RevenueCat. Firebase and RevenueCat work for us; Google also uses advertising data for its own advertising services. Several of them process data in the United States and elsewhere, backed by tools such as standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Nobody receives your data in exchange for money.

Your rights

  • Ask what we hold about your player number and receive a copy.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it, or to restrict or stop its use.
  • Withdraw consent at any time without affecting what happened before.
  • Complain to your data protection authority.

There are no accounts, so the only handle we have on your data is the player number. Write from the address given under Contact and quote the number shown in Settings; we reply within 30 days. The same steps appear on the support page.

Regional notes (GDPR, UK, California)

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR we rely on your consent for advertising identifiers and ad measurement, on our legitimate interest in a stable and improving game for statistics and crash reports, and on the contract with you for purchases. Under California and similar US state laws, sharing advertising identifiers with Google may count as "sharing" or "sale" for targeted advertising; you can opt out through Privacy options in the game, your device's ad settings, or by e-mail. We do not knowingly sell personal data for money, and you will not be treated differently for using these rights.

Children

Hexlu is designed for players who are at least 13 and does not target children. If you think a child has been playing, tell us and the matching data will be erased.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections to the providers above. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, but we collect as little as possible and keep no accounts or passwords.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts using a new service or kind of data, we update this page and the date above before the change reaches the game. Material changes will also be mentioned in the update notes.
