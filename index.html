Hexlu: Stained Glass Lanterns

A calm stained glass puzzle game. Mix coloured light, turn the glass, and light every paper lantern at dawn, noon and dusk with one arrangement.

A colour mixing puzzle that changes with the sun

  • 60 handcrafted levels in four regions, from a coast village to a hilltop monastery
  • Mirrors, prisms, filters, combiners and crystals: mix red, green and blue into yellow, cyan, magenta and white
  • A daily puzzle that is the same for everyone, with a streak and a shareable result
  • A village diorama that lights a window for every level you finish
  • No timer, no move limit, no failing; colour-blind mode and 20 languages; plays offline

Questions

Is Hexlu free?

Yes. Every level can be played without paying. Ads appear between levels; one purchase removes them, and rewarded videos are always optional.

Is there a daily puzzle?

Yes. After level 8 a new daily puzzle opens every day, the same for every player, with a streak counter and a daily gift. You can share your result without spoiling the solution.

Does Hexlu work offline?

Yes. The whole game plays offline and your progress stays on your device. No account is needed.

How does the puzzle work?

Place stained glass pieces on a hexagonal board and turn them to send coloured light to paper lanterns. The sun moves from dawn to noon to dusk, and one arrangement has to light every lantern in all three phases.
